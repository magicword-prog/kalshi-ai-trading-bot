# Trading Strategy

## Focus Areas

- **Economics** - Fed policy, GDP, jobs data, inflation, treasury auctions
- **Politics** - US policy, elections, cabinet/agency decisions, legislative outcomes
- **Crypto/Finance** - BTC/ETH price targets, crypto regulatory events, DeFi milestones
- **Sports** - NFL, MLB baseball, and golf prediction markets

## Philosophy

### Financial Markets (Economics, Politics, Crypto)

Macro-driven analysis. Every trade decision must be grounded in macroeconomic fundamentals, not just price patterns or market sentiment.

Key indicators to weigh:
- **Treasury yields** (2Y, 10Y, yield curve shape)
- **Gold and silver trends** (risk-off signals, inflation hedge demand)
- **DXY (US Dollar Index)** strength/weakness
- **Inflation data** (CPI, PCE, PPI)
- **Fed policy** (rate decisions, dot plot, FOMC minutes, Fed speaker commentary)
- **BTC correlation** with risk assets and macro cycles
- **US policy changes** and cabinet member statements (Treasury Secretary, Fed Chair, etc.)

Default to SKIP unless there is a clear macro-driven edge. Require strong fundamental reasoning for financial markets.

### Sports Markets

Data-driven analysis. Trade decisions based on statistical matchups, recent form, and situational factors.

**NFL:**
- Team records, win/loss trends, and momentum
- Key injuries (QB, star players)
- Home/away splits and weather conditions
- Recent performance trends (last 3-5 games)
- Divisional matchup history

**MLB Baseball:**
- Starting pitching matchups (ERA, WHIP, K rate, recent outings)
- Bullpen strength and availability
- Home/away records
- Recent form (last 7-10 games)
- Lineup changes and injuries

**Golf:**
- Player form (last 3-5 tournaments, cuts made, top-10s)
- Course history and fit (course style vs. player strengths)
- World rankings and Strokes Gained data
- Recent tournament results and momentum

For sports markets, require at least 55% confidence before recommending a trade. Look for value where market odds don't reflect recent developments (injuries, lineup changes, momentum shifts).

## Risk Management

- **Max position size:** 3% of portfolio per trade
- **Minimum confidence:** 60% for financial markets, 55% for sports
- **Max daily loss:** 10% of portfolio
- **Max concurrent positions:** 10
- **Kelly fraction:** 0.5 (half-Kelly, moderate sizing)
- **Daily AI budget:** $5.00 (cost control during testing)

## Trade Approval

All trades require Telegram approval before execution. No autonomous order placement.

## Categories to Skip

- **Entertainment** - Low edge, unpredictable, skip entirely
- **Weather** - Low edge, skip entirely
